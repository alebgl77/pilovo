# Support

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/alebgl77/briefoak/issues) for reproducible bugs, installation problems and feature proposals. Include the Briefoak version, Chrome version, operating system, a short sequence of steps, expected behavior and actual behavior. Redact screenshots and use invented prompts.

Do not attach your browser profile, private prompts, exports, credentials or private conversation links. For suspected security problems, read [SECURITY](SECURITY.md) first.

Support is community/maintainer best-effort. There is no staffed help desk, support email or response-time guarantee. Briefoak cannot resolve provider login, subscription, model or quota issues; use the relevant provider's support for those.
